# Art direction: colourful isometric cooperative board game

- Friendly, low-poly, well-lit 3D rather than realism. Visual reference: cheerful console family-board-game style, combined with clear hex-grid strategy controls.
- 3D board: extruded hex garden tiles, slight bevel, warm soft shadows, soft background and readable outlines.
- Colours with icons/patterns: red/pollen (flower), gold/nectar (blossom or droplet), blue/water (water droplet/waves). Colours are never the sole identifier.
- Objects: bee, beekeeper, stylised wooden hive, simple flowering plants, small weather/environment effects, protection emblems.
- Avoid visual clutter and camera motion; orthographic angled view with bounded pan and zoom.
- Animations should communicate game state, not distract: gentle bounce on selection, resource float on collection, crack/fade on damage, clear nonviolent loss/end screens.
- First milestone may use placeholder primitives. Organise later GLB assets in `public/models/` and textures in `public/textures/`.
- School-friendly: readable across laptop/tablet; large controls and short contextual instructions; reduced-motion option.
- Do not copy external third-party game art or logos; reference images are a style guide only.
